# 客户访谈脚本（interview-script）

> 蒸馏自 `interview-script`｜域：探索｜源词数：722

产出能挖到**真实洞察而非观点**的结构化访谈脚本。遵循「The Mom Test」原则——**ask about their life, not your idea**（问他们的生活，不要问你的点子）。

## 必含章节清单（MUST-SECTIONS）

脚本本体固定 **6 段** + 1 个模板：

- [ ] Opening (2-3 min) — 开场
- [ ] Warm-Up: Context & Background (5 min) — 暖场：背景与情境
- [ ] Core Exploration: Jobs to Be Done (15-20 min) — 核心探询：待办任务（含 4 组问题）
- [ ] Probing Techniques — 追问技巧（5 条）
- [ ] The Mom Test Rules — Mom Test 规则（6 条）
- [ ] Wrap-Up (3-5 min) — 收尾
- [ ] Note-taking template — 记录模板（8 个字段）

执行步骤固定 **4 步**：Clarify research objectives → Create the interview script → Customize the script → Include a note-taking template。

### 可选补充段（OPTIONAL，不参与 BLOCK 判定）

- [ ] Domain Context — 领域背景：客户访谈在 Stage 1 (Explore) 中的定位
- [ ] 前置读取：用户若提供文件（personas、hypothesis lists、product briefs、previous interview notes）先读

## 逐段引导问题（原句照抄）

- **Clarify research objectives**（3 问）：团队需要回答什么具体问题？这项研究将支撑什么决策？哪些假设需要验证？
- **Opening (2-3 min)**：自我介绍并说明目的（**learning, not selling** ／是学习，不是推销）；设定预期——"There are no right or wrong answers. We're here to learn from your experience."；征求录音许可（如适用）；确认可用时长。
- **Warm-Up: Context & Background (5 min)**：
  - "Tell me about your role and what a typical day/week looks like."
  - "How long have you been doing [activity related to the product area]?"
  - 目标：建立融洽关系并理解其情境。
- **Core Exploration: Jobs to Be Done (15-20 min)** — **4 组**：
  - **Current situation and behavior**（过去时、具体实例）：
    - "Walk me through the last time you [did the thing we're exploring]. What happened?"
    - "What tools or methods did you use?"
    - "How long did it take? Who else was involved?"
  - **Pain points and frustrations**（观察，不引导）：
    - "What was the hardest part about that?"
    - "If you could wave a magic wand, what would change?"
    - "What have you tried to solve this? What happened?"
  - **Desired outcomes**（用他们的话，不是你的）：
    - "What does 'good' look like for you in this area?"
    - "How would you know if this was working well?"
  - **Willingness to pay / priority**（skin in the game）：
    - "How much time/money do you currently spend on this?"
    - "Have you looked for a better solution? What did you find?"
    - "What would you give up to have this solved?"
- **Probing Techniques**（撞到有意思的线索时用，**5 条**）：
  - **"Tell me more about that"** — 打开任何话题
  - **"Why?"**（温和地问，2-3 次）— 追到根因
  - **"Can you give me a specific example?"** — 从观点转到事实
  - **"What happened next?"** — 跟着故事走
  - **"How did that make you feel?"** — 捕捉情绪强度
- **Wrap-Up (3-5 min)**：
  - "Is there anything I didn't ask that you think is important?"
  - "Who else should I talk to about this?"
  - 感谢对方；说明后续步骤（如有）。
- **Customize the script**：按具体产品域、画像与研究目标调整问题；按可用访谈时长增删段落。

## 输出模板

脚本本体 6 段 + 记录模板（Note-Taking Template，8 字段照抄）：

```markdown
Participant: [Name / ID]
Date: [Date]
Key Jobs: [What they're trying to accomplish]
Current Solution: [What they use today]
Biggest Pain: [Their #1 frustration]
Desired Outcome: [What success looks like]
Willingness to Pay: [How much they invest / would invest]
Surprise Finding: [Something unexpected]
Follow-up: [Next steps]
```

## 关键规则（The Mom Test Rules，6 条照抄）

- Ask about **their life**, not your idea（问他们的生活，不是你的点子）
- Ask about **the past**, not the future — "Would you use X?" is useless（问过去，不问未来；「你会用 X 吗」这种问题毫无价值）
- **Talk less, listen more** — aim for 80/20 split（少说多听，目标 80/20）
- **Never pitch** during the interview（访谈中绝不推销）
- Look for **strong emotions** — they signal real pain or delight（找强烈情绪——它标记真实的痛点或愉悦）
- **Compliments are noise** — "That sounds cool!" tells you nothing（赞美是噪音；「听起来很酷」什么信息都没有）

补充纪律（源 Domain Context）：客户访谈只是持续探索 **Stage 1 (Explore)** 的**一个**信息源，其他来源包括 stakeholder interviews、usage analytics、data analytics、surveys、market trends、SEO/SEM analysis。PM 需要**直接**接触用户、干系人、工程师与设计师——原句：「without proxies」（不经代理人）。**Product Trio**（PM + Designer + Engineer，Teresa Torres）应共同做探索，而非只有 PM。
