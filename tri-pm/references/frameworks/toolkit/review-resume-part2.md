# 产品经理简历审阅（review-resume · 技能态 · 续篇）

> 续 `review-resume.md`｜源词数：1614｜本篇承接说明性散文与示例（结构性 10 条已在主篇逐条保留）

## 各最佳实践示例（来自源文件的弱/强对照）

**BP1 摘要**
- 弱："Innovative product leader with passion for user-centered design"
- 强："Product Manager with 5 years scaling B2B SaaS platforms; led product launches that increased user retention by 35% and grew revenue from $2M to $15M"

**BP2 人称代词**
- 弱："I led the product strategy for three product lines"
- 强："Led product strategy for three product lines, managing $8M budget and cross-functional teams of 20+"

**BP3 简洁**
- 删/合并无量化影响的要点；以可度量结果优先于职责描述。
- 早期 PM（0–3 年）一页可接受；中期（4–8 年）至多 1–2 页。

**BP4 XYZ+S**
- 弱："Improved product roadmap"
- 强："Increased roadmap visibility and prioritization accuracy (X) by 40% completion rate (Y) by implementing quarterly planning cycles and stakeholder reviews (Z), leading to 6-month product launch acceleration for enterprise customers (S)"

**BP6 贴合岗位**
- 从 JD 提取 5–10 个关键技能/要求；例如 JD 强调"user research"，则确保有"开展用户研究、分析发现、落地洞察"的具体要点。

**BP7 产品与商业能力**
- 弱："Skills: User Research, Product Strategy, Analytics"
- 强："Conducted 25+ user interviews and focus groups; analyzed insights to reprioritize roadmap, shifting focus to retention features that reduced churn by 18%"

**BP8 结构顺序**
- Contact Info（姓名/电话/邮箱/LinkedIn/地点）置顶 → Professional Summary（2–3 行）→ Employment History（倒序，占主体）→ Education → Certifications（Reforge/Product School/Pragmatic Marketing 等 PM 相关）→ Technical Skills（SQL/分析/设计工具，可选置末）。

**BP9 应届/转岗**
- 课程："Completed Reforge Product Strategy and Data-Driven Decision Making"
- 实习："Led feature testing and user feedback collection for iOS app, informing roadmap adjustments"
- 个人项目："Built and launched side project [name], acquired 500+ beta users, analyzed retention data to iterate on core features"
- 转岗：用 PM 视角重述原岗位，如"开展市场研究、分析竞品定位、定义 GTM 策略"。

**BP10 标准职称**
- 标准：Product Manager / Senior Product Manager / Product Manager II / APM / Principal Product Manager
- 避免："Product Ninja""Chief Growth Officer"（除非确为头衔）"Product Guru"
- 一致动作动词：Led, Launched, Increased, Reduced, Improved, Implemented
- 每段含：公司名、职位、起止（月-年）、地点（可选）、3–5 要点。

## Important Guidelines（重要准则）

- 语气轻松专业、鼓励正向。
- 避免说"best practice"，改解释对 PM 岗为何重要。
- 引用原文具体短语/要点。
- 有 JD 时反馈向岗位要求倾斜。
- 具体：不只说"加指标"，要说明哪个指标能强化该要点。
- 优先级：简历偏弱时先聚焦最高影响改动。

## Additional Tips for Product Managers（PM 补充提示）

- 指标最关键：每个主要要点都含量化影响（%、增长、节省时间等）。
- Show, don't tell：不说"data-driven"，而用你做过分析的要点的展示。
- 展示跨职能影响：与 Design/Engineering/Marketing/Sales 的协作。
- 含营收或增长指标：PM 常对营收/增长负责，使其可见。
- 易扫读：用排版让简历在 6–10 秒内可快速浏览。
